# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, evidence-based community across cryptography, law, finance and engineering.

- Be courteous; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Back claims with sources or tests; say "I don't know" when you don't.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Do not post others' personal data. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately (SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for conduct that breaks these rules. Report concerns to the contact listed in SECURITY.md.
